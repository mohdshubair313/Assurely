## Eval status

- **CAL-01 — Confidence calibration against rubric-scored traces (OPEN, release gate).** Implement with `backend/eval/rubric_scorer.py` and eval-harness § 8.1, before treating confidence as a probability or using its threshold as a validated delivery policy. Define the target event and score direction first: the current higher-is-more-confident score is a heuristic; under a future correctness-probability interpretation, 0.46 would imply 46% correctness / 54% error, not 46% error. Measure both observed correctness and error rates near 0.46 and across the full score range on a representative labeled holdout set. Required evidence: versioned traces and rubric labels; judge/human disagreement review; reliability bins with sample counts and uncertainty intervals; Brier score, calibration error, and escalation precision/recall at 0.70. Keep the scoring judge blind to confidence, and exclude the rubric's confidence-consistency item from correctness labels to avoid circular validation. Split related profiles/policy versions together to prevent leakage. The eight synthetic starter cases are smoke tests, not real-world calibration evidence.
